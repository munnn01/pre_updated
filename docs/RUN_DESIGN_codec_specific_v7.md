# Codec-specific V7: independent H.264/H.265 rate penalties

## Question and success criterion

CRC-V5 and the canonical-checkpoint crossover showed that alternating a shared
preprocessor between H.264 and H.265 can improve one codec while damaging the
other. V7 tests whether removing cross-codec gradient interference and choosing
the rate constraint per deployment codec produces a reproducible Top-1 BD-rate
gain.

Negative BD-rate is better. A codec branch continues only if its treatment is
negative, beats its paired fixed-beta control by at least 2 percentage points,
and loses no more than 0.05 absolute Top-1 at every QP. The final two-branch
candidate requires both codec branches to be negative and at least one to reach
`<= -15%`.

## Fixed protocol

- Immutable public Stage-1 checkpoint:
  `dieulinhh/crc-v5-t0-lr1-stage1-v1`, SHA-256
  `a2ad614b874d756db4b5db5fb2a0cefbae300353ed3044a3c27652d0f1b0e5f2`.
- One job calibrates one codec only (`ste_alternate=false`). H.264 and H.265
  checkpoints are independent branches and may later be packed behind a hard
  codec switch; they are not claimed as one shared universal preprocessor.
- Within every job, control and treatment fork the exact same source bytes.
  Control is fixed `beta=0.001`; treatment uses the assigned rate constraint.
- Training: 500 steps, five QPs in randomized complete blocks, frozen teachers
  and real codec forward pass.
- Evaluation: fresh deterministic validation shard `0/10`, salt
  `codec-specific-v7-val-v1`, held-out R2Plus1D-18, selected real codec only.
  The test split is not read.

## Factorial and replication

For each codec, a full 2x2 factorial crosses target added-rate `{0%, -15%}`
with dual LR `{0.005, 0.015}`. `t0_lr5` is the exact recipe associated with the
earlier H.265 `-24.26%` screen. Every codec/arm cell is independently replicated
on two Kaggle accounts. Account is the experimental unit; clip rows inside a
run are repeated measurements, not independent treatment replicates.

## Reading the result

Estimate target, dual-LR and interaction effects separately within each codec,
with account/replicate treated as the run-level nuisance term. Do not select a
branch from one replicate alone. If no replicated arm passes, the scalar
penalty hypothesis is closed and the structural subtractive/DCT route becomes
the primary candidate.
